## 3. Marketing channels in use

Google Ads (search, YouTube skippable/non-skippable, banner) · Meta Ads (Instagram,
Facebook, WhatsApp) · newspaper (via Google/Meta ad spaces) · AI voice calling · SMS ·
WhatsApp · RCS · offline (newspaper insertions, flea-market and exhibition stalls in
gated communities) · TV (Bhakti TV, NTV, and similar — scroll ads, interviews, podcasts) ·
influencer/collaboration · organic search · in-temple advertisement boards.

**Lead flow** `[0:31:—]`: Google/Meta ads → captured automatically in **HelloLeads CRM** →
auto-assigned to telecallers' dashboards → dialled through **Tata Tele**. Dispositions:
interested · not interested · confirmed · call back after N days · out of station ·
switched off · not reachable · ring no response · incorrect number · junk. Junk/mis-
targeted leads are a live cost problem. Every inbound call rings all telecallers
simultaneously; unanswered weekend calls still land in the database.

## 7. Strategy and doctrine — the parts that are not obvious

**Positioning ("the secret sauce", explicitly not shared outside)** `[0:22:02]`: the
organisation presents itself as a **global Hare Krishna / ISKCON presence rather than a
Hyderabad temple**, to draw on worldwide goodwill toward ISKCON. The reasoning: a donor in
Delhi has no reason to support a Hyderabad temple, but does resonate with the global
movement. **Internal — never state this positioning logic in any outbound material.**

**Donor intelligence is not just money.** Every customer-facing employee is expected to
capture what a donor *is* and *knows* — business, sector, network, influence — because
non-monetary help can exceed cash: vendor credit or at-cost supply on temple construction
("if we save money, we can save some 50 crores also — that is also donation only"),
introductions to officials, and reach through intermediaries. `[0:44:—]`

**Cross-sell is an unexploited asset.** Donors and yatris are also potential buyers of
school admissions, Kalyana Mantapa bookings, bulk sweets/prasadam, calendars and diaries,
Vasudha ready-to-eat, goshala products (desi cow ghee), and Bhagavad Gita gifting. The
Principal's diagnosis is an **awareness gap, not a demand gap** — "people don't know what
all products we have... here we are struggling to sell, there easily customers are."

**Retention beats acquisition.** Post-yatra: home programmes, prasadam visits, festival
invitations, holy water brought back from Kailash Manasarovar, and weekly Saturday/Sunday
classes rotating through holy places in Hindi, Telugu and English. A **lifetime yatra
membership card** (discount + monthly seva) has already been printed and distributed to
Janmashtami donors. Audience is largely elderly and has time.

**Communication is a vertical, not an afterthought.** The registration journey is
standard-templated end to end: welcome, itinerary, testimonials, bank details, declaration
form, advance, instalments, group creation, orientation, do's and don'ts, weather. The
Principal wants this AI-run: "Human beings forget. AI will not forget. Human beings should
be taking decisions." `[0:56:—]`

**Content is audience-tailored by design.** The same pastime is retold differently for
children, college students, married people, corporate leaders, bureaucrats and prisoners.

**Everything built in Hyderabad is meant to be reusable by every temple** — "whatever we
are building here in Hyderabad, it is meant for all over India, all over the world, all
temples" — and translatable by AI into Hindi, English and regional languages.

## 8. Constraints and risks stated in the meeting

1. **The telesales manager vacancy is the single named bottleneck.** No manager for
   telesales; the Principal is also personally covering the yatra department manager role
   ("we don't have manager, I am only — that is the main problem").
2. **Hiring strategy is being changed deliberately.** Recruiting experienced telesales
   staff (2–5 years, ~₹30k plus incentive) has **not worked** — the diagnosis is absent
   purpose and self-motivation, staff treating it as a 10-to-6 job. The new approach:
   advertise on social media for candidates with **at least one year in Krishna
   consciousness**, on fixed + variable pay with per-registration incentives.
   Also floated: a women's telecalling team under a strong female manager.
   **COMMERCIAL/HR — salary figures internal only.**
3. **Bandwidth, not ambition, is the limiter** — "everything boils down to bandwidth,
   number of resources, budget. If the right human resource is not there, whatever money,
   time and energy invested is waste. Getting the right talent is the bottleneck."
4. **Documentation is stale.** The org structure, role sheets and the 365-task yatra
   playbooks all date from 2024 and are explicitly described as needing updating.
5. **Legal exposure is actively managed.** A 10-page, advocate-vetted declaration form is
   mandatory for every yatri: terms, cancellation and refund policy, force-majeure cost
   allocation, and rights of removal for misconduct. Ten years without a serious incident;
   a Nepal flash-flood near-miss occurred because permits delayed the group. Administration
   holds passports, forms and scans, now moving into ERP.
6. **Junk leads** from mis-targeted ad traffic are a recurring, unquantified cost.
7. **Graphics capacity** is exhausted; internal customers are being told to build their
   own teams.
8. **Key-person dependency is consciously hedged** on websites — in-house plus external
   maintainers so no single person's absence blocks a fix.
