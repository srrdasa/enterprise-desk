# Voice & Preferences — the Principal's writing, learned from evidence

Rules land here at THREE OR MORE instances sharing a root cause (CLAUDE.md rule 10).
One incident is noise — log it in logs/edits.jsonl and wait for the pattern.

Before drafting anything in the Principal's name: read his actual sent mail (both
mailboxes), his Slack messages, and the prior thread with that recipient. Match
greeting, directness, length and register — partner, vendor, staff and leadership are
different registers. Style adapts; rigour does not.
